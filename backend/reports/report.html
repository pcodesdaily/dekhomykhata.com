MyKhata · transaction categoriser · model report

Reading Indian bank statements the way you would

The model turns cryptic narrations like UPI/DR/412345678901/BUNDL TE/YESB into one of 26 spending categories, says so when it is unsure, and runs in about 400 µs per transaction from a 1.5 MB file. Every number on this page is produced by scripts/train.py.

Why this model

  • Most accurate on unseen brands and the real statement, and within 0.3 points of the best on known brands (Char + word TF-IDF + linear SVM, which cannot say 'unsure'). 41 points ahead of the brand keyword lookup that most expense apps use.
  • Knows when it doesn't know. Its confidence is honest (calibration error 2.6%), so low-confidence rows become "Uncategorised" instead of silently wrong numbers in your budget. The SVM and keyword rules cannot do this.
  • Understands Indian statement formats. Character n-grams read truncated names (BUNDL TE), UPI IDs and legal entity names, and the amount and debit/credit direction separate a refund from a purchase.
  • Private and cheap to run. No external AI API sees your transactions: the model runs on your own server in 400 µs per row, from a 1.5 MB file with an integrity check.
  • Checked against the bank's own numbers. The statement reader reconciles every row with the running balance, and savings are computed from money in and out, not from labels.

Where it is strong and where it is weak

Accuracy on the unseen-brand test, by who is on the other side of the transaction. Brands the model has never seen are the weak spot: nothing in a name like STARBUCKS says "coffee". Adding brands to the catalogue is what fixes it.

Training data

No public labelled dataset of Indian personal statements exists, so training data is generated in the formats of SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PSU banks and app exports, with 887 brands (1,272 name variants), weighted by popularity.

462 rows whose text maps to several categories (e.g. a bare NEFT) were relabelled "Uncategorised".

Limits

  • Training data is synthetic; formats were reconstructed, not copied from every bank's real statements.
  • The real statement was also used to find gaps in the generator, so its score is optimistic. A second, untouched real statement is needed.
  • Brands missing from the catalogue fall to about 62% accuracy.
  • Scanned (photographed) PDFs are not supported yet.
